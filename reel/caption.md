# Instagram reel: Asthma Alley After the Toll

- **Video:** `asthma-alley-reel.mp4` (1080×1920, 30 fps, 34 s, H.264/AAC)
- **Cover:** `cover.png` (the "ALL 15" frame). Set it under *Edit cover → Add from camera roll*.

The beat and every sound effect are generated by the script, so the audio is
original and can't be muted or blocked for copyright.

## Posting tips

- **Trending audio:** the reel ships with its own beat, which is safe to post as is. To ride a trending
  sound as well, add it in Instagram's editor at about 10 to 20% volume and keep the original audio at
  100%. That gets the sound's discovery boost and keeps the sound effects in sync.
- **Burned-in captions:** the on-screen text carries the story, so it works with the sound off.
- **Safe zones:** all text sits between y≈250 and y≈1500, clear of Instagram's bottom caption and right-side buttons.
- **Collab:** invite your team accounts as collaborators so the reel shows on every profile.
- **Timing:** post on a weekday evening (6 to 9 pm ET) and reply to comments in the first hour.
- **Stories:** share the reel to Stories with a link sticker pointing to the map.
